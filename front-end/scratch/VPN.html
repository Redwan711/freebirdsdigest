How We Evaluated These VPNs

We evaluated the five VPNs across nine categories:

  1. Privacy and security
  2. Speed and performance
  3. Streaming
  4. Features
  5. Network coverage
  6. Usability
  7. Device support
  8. Price and value
  9. Customer support

Privacy and security received the highest weight because a VPN's fundamental purpose is to protect internet traffic and improve privacy.

Performance and streaming received the next-highest weights because they have a direct impact on everyday usability.

Prices were considered important, but we did not allow promotional pricing alone to determine the winner.

Important: VPN Pricing Changes

VPN companies frequently change promotional prices.

The price shown in an advertisement may depend on:

  • subscription length
  • promotional campaign
  • country
  • taxes
  • coupon
  • new-customer status
  • renewal period
  • plan tier

For that reason, always check the provider's current checkout price before purchasing.

A low introductory price should not automatically be treated as the long-term cost.

Are VPNs Worth It?

For many users, a VPN can be useful for improving privacy on public Wi-Fi, changing the IP address visible to websites, securing internet traffic, and accessing services while traveling.

But a VPN isn't a complete online-security solution.

It does not make you anonymous, prevent every type of tracking, or protect you from phishing and scams by itself.

Good account security, strong passwords, two-factor authentication, software updates, and careful browsing still matter.
