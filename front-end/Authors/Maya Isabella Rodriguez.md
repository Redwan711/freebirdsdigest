# **Short Biography:**

Maya Rodriguez is a digital wellbeing researcher and technology lifestyle writer specializing in the relationship between humans and technology. Her work explores how people can build healthier relationships with digital devices while maintaining productivity, creativity, and personal balance.

With a background in behavioral science, digital communication, and workplace wellbeing, Maya studies the psychological impact of technology usage, remote work habits, notification overload, and modern digital lifestyles.

She believes technology should enhance human life rather than control it. Through research-based articles, interviews, and practical frameworks, Maya helps professionals, freelancers, and entrepreneurs create healthier digital routines.

Her writing focuses on finding balance between productivity and wellbeing in an increasingly connected world.

# **About Section:**

Technology has transformed the way we work, communicate, and live. My work focuses on one important question: how can we use technology without allowing it to negatively impact our wellbeing?

I am a digital wellbeing researcher and writer specializing in human technology interaction, workplace habits, and healthier digital lifestyles.

Over the past decade, I have studied how professionals, entrepreneurs, and remote workers interact with technology in their daily lives. My research explores topics such as digital overload, notification management, remote work balance, and creating sustainable productivity systems.

I believe technology itself is not the problem. The challenge is building intentional relationships with the tools we use every day.

Through research-based writing, workplace programs, and educational content, I help individuals and organizations create healthier digital environments.

My goal is to bridge the gap between technology innovation and human wellbeing by promoting a more balanced, thoughtful approach to digital life.
