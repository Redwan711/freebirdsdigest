# **Short Biography:**

Daniel Okafor is a personal finance writer specializing in money management for digital professionals, freelancers, and small business owners. With a background in financial analysis and business education, he focuses on helping people understand income management, budgeting, savings, and sustainable wealth-building.

His writing explores the unique financial challenges faced by independent workers, including irregular income, pricing services, managing taxes, and building emergency funds.

Daniel believes financial literacy is a critical skill for the modern digital economy. His mission is to make personal finance concepts simple, practical, and accessible for professionals around the world.
